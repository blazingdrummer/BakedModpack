## ArtificerPrimaryVFX

Artificer's gauntlets play a sound and VFX when regaining primary stocks.

Client-side and Vanilla-compatible.

Sound can be toggled in the config.

## For Devs

You can add support for your custom primary skills by registering your skill in `ArtificerPrimaryVfxPlugin.SkillMappings`.

`ArtificerPrimaryVfxPlugin.Vfx` provides a quick reference for Vanilla VFX to use.
