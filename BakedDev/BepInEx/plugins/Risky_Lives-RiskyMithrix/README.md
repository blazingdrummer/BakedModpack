## RiskyMithrix

A mod that buffs Mithrix against mobile/airborne players while keeping his general moveset recognizable.

Rather than being a challenge mod that completely reworks Mithrix, this mod aims to be a general-use mod that can easily slot into any modpack.

All changes can be toggled in the config.

## Changes

- General
	- Health increased from 1000 -> 1200
	
		*Higher than current, but not as high as the Anniversary Update (1400) where he felt too tanky.*
		
	- 50% resistance to Move Slow and Attack Speed Reduction.
	- Fall Damage Immunity.
	- Freeze resistance.
	- Prioritizes players.
	
- Hammer Slam
	- Momentum is now stopped when using this skill.
	
		*High movement speed causes Mithrix to slide and constantly miss his hammer attacks.*
		
	- Mithrix rotates towards his target before using this skill.
	
		*Another fix geared towards Mithrix's terrible hammer accuracy.*
		
	- Spawns 3 moving flame pillars that travels forwards.
		
		*This is Mithrix's main attack, and it gets completely hard-countered by any sort of height difference. Flame Pillars should help with that.*

- Sprinting/Phase 4 Lunar Shards replaced with Lunar Orbs
	
	*In Mithrix's normal phases, the shards never hit and barely do any damage. In Phase 4, the shards are impossible to visually read and can often lead to frustrating deaths. This projectile deals much more damage than the shards and has more reliable homing and AoE, but is easier for players to read and react to.*
		
- Hammer Bash
	- Shoots a fan spread of Lunar Shards.
	
		*Gives him some minor poke damage when out of melee range.*
		
	- No longer can trimp.
	
		*Mithrix is completely helpless while airborne, and this skill can often launch him into the ceiling.*
		
- Dash
	- Backwards dash no longer scales with move speed.
	
		*This often will lead to him dashing out of hammer range, then immediately using the hammer uselessly.*
		
- Sky Leap
	- Targets players, instead of the center of the arena.
	
		*The vanilla behavior ends up making him super vulnerable and predictable, and is actively detrimental to him when fighting people camping on ramps/the sides of the arena.*
	
	- Spawns flame pillars on his position upon landing.
		- Spawns more pillars during Phase 3.
		
		*So that this skill doesn't get hard-countered by simply floating in the air.*
